How it works

From your door and back in 72 hours

  1. Send photos

    Message us photos of your pair. We tell you which service it needs and the price, for free.

  2. We pick them up

    Choose one of our fixed pickup days. We photograph the pair and note its condition.

  3. Hand cleaning

    Each pair is cleaned by hand and dried slowly, without a washing machine.

  4. Back at your door

    Within 72 hours, with before-and-after photos and a note of what we used.

FAQ

Questions

Which materials do you clean?

Canvas, mesh, smooth leather, suede and nubuck. For rare or very delicate materials, we check photos first and tell you honestly if we would not take the risk.

What if something goes wrong with my pair?

We photograph every pair at pickup and test products on a hidden spot first. If we damage a pair, we are responsible for it, and the service will be insured for customers' items.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Prague. Pickup and delivery run on fixed days each week, so we can keep the price low.

Which products do you use?

Water-based cleaners chosen for each material. We tell you exactly which product we used on your pair.
