FAQ

Questions

Which materials do you clean?

Canvas, mesh, leather, suede and nubuck. For rare materials, we check photos first.

What if my pair gets damaged?

Every pair is photographed at pickup and products are tested on a hidden spot first. If we damage a pair, we are responsible, and the service will be insured.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Prague, on fixed days each week.

Which products do you use?

Water-based cleaners chosen for each material. We tell you what we used.
